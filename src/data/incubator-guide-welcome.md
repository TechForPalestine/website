## Attributes of Successful projects

We’ve noticed some key attributes of projects that are successful within T4P.

- They focus on users early and often
- They address things they don’t know how to do (by learning or getting volunteers)
- They come to office hours often at the start, sometimes multiple times per week
- They build teams — though not too early — and build leaders within those teams
- They listen to our advice — this isn’t to say that they always implement the advice, but if you come to an Office Hours to fight with mentors, you probably won’t succeed.

Please also check messages and notifications in T4P. We communicate with you by Discord usually, so set Discord notifications up so that you get them. Also frequently check your email spam folder (and add our addresses to your contact book). Otherwise you’ll miss a good introduction, a good opportunity, a good volunteer, upcoming events etc.

Additionally, you have a public channel on our discord, and sometimes excited potential users or volunteers come by.

## The Mechanics of the Incubator

### Discord Server

As a project leader you will have access to:

- our private area on discord
  - #[project-leaders channel](https://discord.com/channels/1186702814341234740/1238877766804443146)
- a private channel for your project - you, your mentor, and the management team have access here. It’s to coordinate T4P resources
- A public channel for your product - useful to answer questions from the community, share announcements, etc.

### Initial integration

New projects will begin their journey within the Incubator with a one-hour strategy session with Paul to set initial direction and priorities. After that, projects will officially join the T4P Incubator and will enter a six-week onboarding phase, a period designed to build momentum, gain early feedback, and establish a strong presence within the T4P community.

During this time, projects are expected to attend office hours at least once a week to stay connected and receive ongoing support. If the current office hour times aren’t convenient, please let us know so we can schedule a dedicated session.

### Office Hours

We hold regular office hours calls to get feedback on your project, and meet other leaders. Project leaders should drop-in at least once a week for their first 6 weeks in the incubator, then at least every 2 weeks until they reach product market fit. After that, we recommend visiting monthly.

Office hours take place in a 1:1 setting and are booked through your ProjectHub.

- Usually you speak to Paul for 15 minutes
- Excellent for motivation and sharing learnings
- Discuss challenges
- Meet other project leaders
- Get resources and advice from T4P management

Office hours are extremely important, please do not skip them.

### Feedback & advice

Paul offers feedback and advice to Projects during the Office Hours calls. Highly recommended (and we actually require attendance at certain intervals - once a week while you are new). This includes:

- Feedback on the vision, goals, roadmap
- Are you working on the right thing?
- Getting initial feedback and users
- How to get more impact
- How to build software
- How to launch

### Volunteers

We have over 3000 volunteers in our database, and they’re dying to help. Volunteers have diverse skill sets - think of different ways to integrate them in your project:

- help lead subparts of your project
- a team member that does regular work (eg coding, or coordination, or research)
- a co-founder or partner in running your project
- do one off research or data entry tasks

We have found that simply adding dedicated and motivated team members can be extremely valuable, even if you don’t have specific roles for them as yet. We periodically advertise the incubator projects to our volunteers. We will direct any interested volunteers your way. Once you evaluate them, you can use their help in the project in one of many ways: as team members, users, or just to build a community around the project.

See [Working with T4P Volunteers](/incubator/guide/working-with-volunteers) for more details

### Users

We can help you get some initial users.

- This may involve a small post on social media or within Discord to get some early users to help with user development
- If your project is ready for a big launch and if everyone agrees this is the right time, we are usually able to help coordinate a bigger launch on multiple socials, possibly including with partners and media.
