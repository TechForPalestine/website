<p>Wikipedia is under attack from the Israel Lobby and its allies in the U.S. government, who are running a campaign providing “sustained, multi-front external pressure on the platform”. <strong>The goal of this attack is to gain influence over Wikipedia, which would change the results of AI platforms such as ChatGPT to be more favorable to Israel.</strong></p>
<p>Israel has long relied on propaganda to maintain its global image. That image is undermined by public awareness of its history of crimes against humanity in Palestine, particularly its recent genocide in Gaza. <strong>A key propaganda target for Israel is Wikipedia, on which much of the world relies for information about Israel and Palestine. Wikipedia’s data reaches the public not just through Wikipedia, but also through Google Search and AI chatbots such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Thaura, which are built using Wikipedia.</strong></p>
<p>Over the last 20 years, <strong>18</strong> publicly-known Israel-aligned groups have conspired to make Wikipedia more pro-Israel, <strong>9</strong> of which are still active. However, these attacks have had limited effect because of Wikipedia’s strong editorial controls and reliance on a consensus of scholarly work.</p>
<p>Israel is stepping up the attack, especially by using the Israel Lobby to gain influence over Wikipedia. The ADL, joined by AIPAC-funded members of Congress, have weaponized false claims to gain influence over Wikipedia’s parent, the Wikimedia Foundation.</p>
<p>The ADL released a deeply flawed report in March 2025, titled “Editing for Hate”, which claims mass collusion to introduce antisemitism on Wikipedia. Dr. Shira Klein, an expert on antisemitism and Wikipedia who is cited in the report, rebutted it:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>“But where is the evidence of editors repeatedly misrepresenting or contradicting peer-reviewed literature? There is none. The report simply wants us to take the ADL’s word for it”.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>Within this report, we analyze each of the claims in the ADL’s report, identifying significant and widespread flaws. No claim survived scrutiny, due to methodological flaws, cherry-picked evidence, misunderstanding of Arabic content, manipulation and misrepresentation of evidence, and inconsistencies in their own data. The ADL also created fake-scientific terminology to fabricate evidence, and frame ordinary Wikipedia activity as sinister.</p>
<p>In fact, the ADL did not show that antisemitism was introduced to Wikipedia, nor a single false fact. The collusion itself is also likely fabricated: the ADL picked editors they didn’t like, and made meaningless statistical correlations to fit their pre-defined narrative of collusion, coupled with definitively-false claims.</p>
<p>The stated goals of the ADL’s report are to influence the Wikimedia Foundation to change Wikipedia’s editing policy, introducing vectors for the ADL to influence Wikipedia’s content. These suggestions, such as a task force on antisemitism, or a board of experts on Israel and Palestine, sound innocuous, but offer the Israel Lobby the potential to exert editorial control over Wikipedia content.</p>
<p>Another goal of the ADL is to influence AI companies drop, Wikipedia’s high-quality, factual data. This data relies on a consensus of scholarly opinion, replacing it with news articles from U.S. media, which typically contain significant pro-Israel bias.</p>
<p>The ADL’s March 2025 report was used in 4 U.S. government attacks on Wikipedia, one from a bipartisan group of 23 members of Congress, another by House Committee chairs Comer and Mace, and another by Senate Committee chair Ted Cruz. Each of these politicians was AIPAC-funded, to a total of $96M.</p>
<p>Further attacks have threatened Wikimedia’s charitable status, and targeted Middle East Studies departments and programs at U.S. universities, whose research form the scholarly basis of much of Wikipedia’s content on Palestine and Israel.</p>
<p>A similar attack involved an internal Wikipedia arbitration case, where a pro-Israel Wikipedia editor launched a well-resourced attack on editors they deemed pro-Palestine, gaining sanctions including editing bans against six of them.</p>
<p>The ADL has also revealed that they are working with AI platforms, including Anthropic and OpenAI, to directly manipulate how those AI platforms respond. It appears they also have access to the source code of AI chatbots such as ChatGPT and Claude.</p>
<p>Further attacks are coming. The pro-Israel Heritage Foundation announced plans to dox anonymous Wikipedia editors as part of “Project Esther”. The pro-Israel neoconservative institution Foundation for Defense of Democracies is working with NPOV, an Israel-based “Wikipedia intelligence” company, to stalk Wikipedia editors. They are also working with the ADL who has its own group of 45 analysts “tracking” Wikipedia editors. Doxxed editors have received death threats.</p>
<p>The ADL has claimed success in their campaign. In “Damage Done”, an October 2026 report, the ADL celebrated its attacks, calling them “sustained, multi-front external pressure on the platform”, with pressure coming from “congressional scrutiny”, “federal inquiries”, “Senate Commerce Committee, enforcement work by Wikipedia’s NPOV-policy editors, ADL reporting, and broader media scrutiny”.</p>
<p>This new 2026 report also set the stage for more attacks on Wikipedia, demanding the establishment of a task force on antisemitism, a common tactic by the Israel Lobby, despite not having demonstrated a single piece of antisemitism on Wikipedia. They further ask for a review of each edit introduced by banned editors, though the editors were not banned for introducing false information onto Wikipedia, nor has the ADL demonstrated that they have done so.</p>
<p>We sound the alarm on this propaganda attack on Wikipedia and on AI, and urge Wikimedia to understand that it is being attacked by nation-state resources and to act accordingly. In particular, we believe it is essential that Wikimedia move out of the U.S. We further recommend that the new Congress investigate and censure past attacks from their colleagues on Wikipedia, and that the media increase their scrutiny on dishonest attacks by the Israel Lobby.</p>

<h2 id="about-this-report">About this Report</h2>
<p>This report began in March 2025 after the release of the ADL’s biased and falsified report, entitled “Editing for Hate: How Anti-Israel and Anti-Jewish Bias Undermines Wikipedia’s Neutrality”. Being familiar with Israel’s history of directly manipulating Wikipedia, we began to investigate whether the report itself may be part of an influence operation to attack Wikipedia.</p>
<p>Narrative control is essential for Israel. Knowing the extensive resources Israel dedicates to propaganda, the role of the ADL and AIPAC in the Israel Lobby to create influence in the U.S., and Israel’s repeated attempts to directly manipulate Wikipedia, the ADL’s report made clear that this was the start of a campaign to gain influence over Wikipedia, and thus, also over AI.</p>
<p>As various AIPAC-aligned U.S. politicians began to attack Wikipedia using the ADL’s report, we felt it was essential to explain how various elements of the Israel Lobby and their allies were working together to attack Wikipedia.</p>
<p>This report features three tracks:</p>
<ul>
<li><p>In Sections <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-3">3</a> and <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-4">4</a>, we document Israel's coordinated efforts to influence Wikipedia in order to enable the general public to recognize how these operations seek to advance Israeli interests in society,</p></li>
<li><p>In Section <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-6">6</a>, we dismantle the low-quality, and frequently manipulated evidence presented by the ADL in their attempt to create false claims of antisemitism with which to influence Wikipedia,</p></li>
<li><p>In Sections <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-2">2</a> and <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-5">5</a> we explain the tools of Israeli propaganda as they relate to the attack on Wikipedia.</p></li>
</ul>
<p>Our goal is a strong commons, one based on facts and truth, and not controlled, influenced, or manipulated by propaganda or state actors.</p>
