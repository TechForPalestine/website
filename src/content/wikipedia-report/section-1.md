<p>Wikipedia is under attack from the Israel Lobby and its allies in the U.S. government, who are running a campaign providing “sustained, multi-front external pressure on the platform”,<sup class="fn-ref"><a href="#fn-1" id="fnref-1">1</a></sup> which we describe in <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-4">Section 4</a>.</p>
<p>Significantly, in <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-6">Section 6</a>, we offer a detailed rebuttal of the central artifact in the attack on Wikipedia—a 2025 report produced by the Anti-Defamation League (ADL). This deeply flawed, embarrassing piece of propaganda lacks any credibility, and we outline its various half-truths, argumentative fallacies, and unverifiable accusations. We debunk the report, addressing each of the false claims and contorted logic used by the ADL, as well as the report’s reliance on a series of biased definitions and fabricated terminology, with which the ADL attempts to misrepresent the truth. Our rebuttal also addresses the ADL’s follow-up to the report, published in October 2026, in which the ADL confesses to what it calls “sustained, multi-front external pressure on the platform”.</p>
<p>Israel is currently running an influence operation on Wikipedia, intending to manipulate Wikipedia’s factual content about Palestine-Israel. The aim of the operation is to manufacture a narrative about Wikipedia, and rally allies in order to weaponize that narrative.</p>
<p>The Israel Lobby’s attacks on Wikipedia are dishonest, and seek to secure Israeli influence over Wikipedia’s content and its editing process. These attacks also seek to hide the history of Israel’s violent occupation of Palestine, by inserting a pro-Israel narrative into Wikipedia.</p>
<p>In this report, we examine the influence attack against Wikipedia, along with its different components, and refute the central document on which they rely: a biased and falsified report produced by the Anti-Defamation League (ADL).<sup class="fn-ref"><a href="#fn-2" id="fnref-2">2</a></sup> We outline both the attacks and their intended purpose, relying on well-sourced publicly available documentation.</p>
<p>Attacking Wikipedia is important for Israel as they seek to maintain their long-time narrative control over their 78-year violent occupation of Palestine. However, Israel’s genocide in Gaza following their 2023 invasion have undermined Israel’s narrative control, revealing the truth about Israel’s occupation, violence against Palestinians, and apartheid in Palestine.</p>
<p>Footage of Israel’s genocide in Gaza, shared on social media platforms, has introduced people around the world to the reality of Israel’s continued violent occupation of Palestine. Many who are seeking to learn the truth about Israel find it on Wikipedia, or other knowledge bases derived from Wikipedia, including ChatGPT and Google Search.</p>
<p>It is no secret that Israel invests heavily into propaganda in order to shape perceptions of Israel around the world. Since the founding of the Israeli state, it has sought to promote its own narratives about its history in order to maintain dominance and the apartheid system, in which Palestinians are systematically killed, tortured, imprisoned, and displaced from their land. This propaganda is also used to justify their ongoing violence against Palestinians, including the Gaza genocide.</p>
<p>These attacks on Wikipedia follow nearly 20 years of Israeli attempts to manipulate Wikipedia directly. Wikipedia’s robust and mature mechanisms for quality control and editorial oversight have prevented these manipulations from having significant impact. Having failed to control the narrative on Wikipedia, a number of Israel-aligned organizations and politicians have coordinated to attack Wikipedia and Wikipedia editors, as well as its parent non-profit, the Wikimedia Foundation itself.</p>
<h2 id="s1-1">1.1 Structure of the report</h2>
<p>In this report, we document this attack on Wikipedia, analyzing the motivations behind the attack, its actors, and the false evidence produced.</p>
<p><strong>In <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-2">Section 2</a></strong>, we explain the purpose of Israel’s attack on Wikipedia.</p>
<p>We provide an overview of Israel’s history, and specifically its ongoing violence against the Palestinian people. We expose the motivation behind Israel’s propaganda movement, which seeks to obscure and erase evidence of their crimes. This propaganda movement holds significant influence throughout Western society, and especially in the U.S.</p>
<p>Due to its long-term use of propaganda, Israel has gained significant influence in politics, the media, and the arts across the West, but not on Wikipedia. We recognize Wikipedia's resilience in resisting these efforts, thanks in part to its well-established editing system, which relies on scholarly sources and a neutral point of view. This has allowed the platform to remain largely resistant to the Israeli propaganda machine.</p>
<p>We provide background on Israel’s recent stated propaganda goal of manipulating GenAI/LLMs such as ChatGPT to produce content more favorable to the pro-Israel position, and Wikipedia’s place in that project.</p>
<p>We also introduce Israel’s language of propaganda, centered around fighting criticism of Israel. We discuss the flawed IHRA definition, which conflates criticism of Israel with antisemitism, and the new term “pro-Hamas”, used to tie criticism of Israel to terrorism. These tools and terminology of the Israeli propaganda movement are repeatedly used by U.S. lawmakers, as we discuss in <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-4">Section 4</a>, and by the ADL, as discussed in Sections <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-5">5</a> and <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-6">6</a>.</p>
<p>In <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-3">Section 3</a>, we document no less than 18 different pro-Israel initiatives to directly manipulate Wikipedia, including manipulation initiatives by the ADL itself, and one led by former Israeli Prime Minister Naftali Bennett. As of November 2026, 8 of these initiatives appear to be ongoing.</p>
<p>In <strong><a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-4">Section 4</a>,</strong> we document the influence attack against Wikipedia.</p>
<p>We discuss the various U.S. politicians who have attacked Wikipedia as part of Israel’s influence campaign, and those who have taken bribes from the Israel Lobby. These include 23 AIPAC-aligned members of Congress, led by U.S. Representative Debbie Wasserman Schultz; Ed Martin, the interim U.S. Attorney for Washington D.C.; and the AIPAC-aligned chairs of the House Committee on Oversight and Government Reform, the House Subcommittee on Cybersecurity, Information Technology, and Government Innovation, and Ted Cruz, acting as chair of the Senate Committee on Commerce, Science and Transportation.</p>
<p>We also discuss the surveillance operation on Wikipedia editors in <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-4#s4-5">Section 4.5</a>, outlining how multiple organizations are surveilling Wikipedia editors, including the Heritage Foundation, the ADL, a private Israeli-led company led by one of the instigators of the attacks, and Builders for Tomorrow, the “stealth nonprofit startup” identified as a partner by the ADL. We also discuss which U.S. and European laws these organizations may be breaking, according to their own documentation.</p>
<p>In <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-4#s4-6">Section 4.6</a>, we review the recent appointment of Wikimedia’s new CEO, and her past as a U.S. diplomat, and in particular her close collaboration with pro-Israel organizations as U.S. Ambassador to Chile. We question whether her appointment is a result of the influence attack, which occurred during Wikimedia’s search for a CEO.</p>
<p>And in <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-4#s4-7">Section 4.7</a>, we connect Israel’s attacks on Wikipedia to the U.S. government’s attacks on U.S. universities, which are a major producer of the scholarly work on which Wikipedia relies.</p>
<p>In <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-5">Section 5</a>, we discuss the ADL itself. The ADL is a pro-Israel lobby group masquerading as a civil rights organization. The ADL’s report, “Editing for Hate: How Anti-Israel and Anti-Jewish Bias Undermines Wikipedia’s Neutrality”, is a deliberate hit piece on Wikipedia, serving as the core narrative that has been cited and used to pressure and influence Wikimedia, the parent organization of Wikipedia.</p>
<p>We examine the ADL’s role as a pro-Israel lobby group and provide context for why an organization ostensibly dedicated to civil rights would fabricate a report attacking Wikipedia. We demonstrate via the ADL’s own claims and actions that it is a pro-Israel propaganda organization, one which uses claims of antisemitism to protect Israel from criticism and expand Israeli influence. The ADL is a well-known part of the Israel Lobby, one which has been deemed unreliable by Wikipedia on the subject of Israel and antisemitism, and which has been criticized as bigoted and untrustworthy by civil society groups, non-profits, and NGOs.</p>
<p>We argue that the ADL frequently violates its own stated values in order to fight for Israel, weaponizing claims of antisemitism to silence opposition and increase Israeli influence in digital spaces. Its report attacking Wikipedia is presented as part of this broader effort: a propaganda tool intended to influence Wikipedia and promote a pro-Israel and anti-human-rights agenda.</p>
<p>In <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-6">Section 6</a>, we closely examine the ADL’s report, the primary piece of evidence used by various Israel-aligned politicians and agencies alleging discrimination, antisemitism, and anti-Israel bias in Wikipedia.</p>
<p>In <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-6#s6-1">Sections 6.1</a> through Section <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-6#s6-5">6.5</a>, we explain how the claims made in the ADL’s report are nonsensical, biased, and factually incorrect. <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-6#s6-1">Section 6.1</a> outlines the reports’ core flaws. <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-6#s6-2">Section 6.2</a> discusses an article from antisemitism and Wikipedia expert Dr. Shira Klein, which refutes the ADL’s report.</p>
<p><a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-6#s6-3">Section 6.3</a> presents our methodical analysis of Case Study 1, in which the ADL claims coordinated collusion among Wikipedia editors, finding almost every claim to have no factual basis. This section provides significant evidence for our conclusion, that the ADL’s report is simply a dressed-up piece of propaganda.</p>
<p><a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-6#s6-4">Section 6.4</a> extends the same analysis of Case Study 2, where the ADL relies on overly-simplified translations of Arabic-language Wikipedia content, interpreting this content without linguistic or cultural context, and through its biased lens.</p>
<p>In <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-6#s6-5">Section 6.5</a>, we review a follow-up report by the ADL, entitled “Damage Done: Wikipedia Leaves Biased Edits Unchecked Despite Editor Bans”. In it, the ADL celebrates how they and their allies manipulated Wikipedia. They make additional false claims, which identify a supposed new cohort of antisemitic editing, restate old recommendations, and make new ones, in order to gain more influence over the platform. We dissect this report, methodically examining its spin and narrative claims that are once again unsupported by evidence, as well as its goals and purpose as propaganda.</p>
<p>In <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-6#s6-6">Section 6.6</a>, we challenge a major claim of the ADL, that there exists “systematic editing” of Wikipedia, supposedly orchestrated by Tech for Palestine, the authors of this report. This claim is false, and contradicted by the ADL’s own cited sources. This false claim itself demonstrates how the ADL works with other propagandists to manufacture narratives that it later weaponizes.</p>
<p>In <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-6#s6-7">Section 6.7</a>, we go through the ADL’s recommendations, demonstrating that they are based on the false evidence from their report, and showing how they are attempting to weaponize the report for influence over Wikipedia, and over future LLM and AI technologies.</p>
<p>Finally, in <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-7">Section 7</a>, we recommend a series of actions for the Wikimedia Foundation, the U.S. and other governments, the Jewish community, LLM providers, civil rights organizations, and the ADL itself, including its donors and partners. We also offer recommendations for the media in their coverage of Israel’s propaganda attacks on Wikipedia and elsewhere.</p>
