<h2 id="s7-1">7.1 Recommendations to Wikimedia Foundation</h2>
<h3 id="s7-1-1">7.1.1 Stand up to the Attack</h3>
<p>We believe it is massively important for Wikipedia to stand up to the assault on them. They must recognize that this is not a standard attack, but rather an orchestrated assault with nation-state resources, which has the potential to turn them from a place that contains and organizes knowledge, to one where knowledge is subjugated to spin from the interests of propaganda movements.</p>
<p>Capitulating to the Israel Lobby is a massive risk, as this will open the doors for many other future propaganda and misinformation campaigns and organizations to coordinate with the U.S. administration and Congress.</p>
<p>The efforts of the ADL report and the Heritage Foundation clearly reveal how deep we truly are in the era of misinformation and disinformation. Wikipedia holds the keys to “truth” in search engines and LLMs, and, as a result, is the biggest target for misinformation and propaganda. We see this already with Israel’s attempts to manipulate Wikipedia into a tool for Israeli propaganda. If Wikimedia does not fight back, Wikipedia as a source of genuine truth will be destroyed.</p>
<h3 id="s7-1-2">7.1.2 Beware of Inside-Outside Strategy</h3>
<p>A common strategy we have observed around Israeli influence is the “inside-outside” strategy. In this approach, outsiders such as the ADL or U.S. government create pressure on an organization. Insiders to that organization use this pressure as a means to push through changes they already sought to implement.</p>
<p>We have noted these tactics being applied within many tech companies. Though ostensibly not pro-Israel in terms of policy, these organizations’ pro-Israel workers weaponize internal policies, alongside false claims of antisemitism, in order to achieve a dominance of pro-Israel narratives internally. This leads to significant changes in company policies, which, in practice, favor pro-Israel narratives in support of the occupation, apartheid and ongoing genocide, over stated company commitments to anti-discrimination and supporting human rights.</p>
<p>Similarly, we discuss in <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-4#s4-7">Section 4.7</a> how many universities have taken on pro-Israel policies after attacks from students, faculty, and ultimately the Trump administration. Insiders such as donors, faculty, board members, and trustees, may have pushed for the same policies, and were able to leverage the Trump Administration attacks to push for pro-Israel policies they already supported.</p>
<p>Pertaining to this case on Wikipedia, we suspect that Israel-aligned insiders in Wikimedia may be working against the goals of Wikipedia, in order to support Israel’s attempts to gain influence over Wikipedia. Wikimedia should work to ensure such influence cannot happen, and investigate whether it is in fact occurring. We look in particular at Jimmy Wales (in <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-7#s7-1-6">Section 7.1.6</a>) and new CEO Bernadette Meehan (in <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-7#s7-1-7">Section 7.1.7</a>).</p>
<h3 id="s7-1-3">7.1.3 Move out of the United States</h3>
<p>We believe these political attacks will continue, something the Wikimedia Foundation is well aware of.<sup class="fn-ref"><a href="#fn-449" id="fnref-449">449</a></sup> They estimate the cost as being upwards of “tens to hundreds” of millions of dollars, a small price to pay to ensure Wikipedia is safe.</p>
<p>As tech slides into the U.S. autocratic regime, the Wikimedia Foundation is faced with a difficult situation in which its corporate structure and environment are subject to influence that goes against Wikimedia values.<sup class="fn-ref"><a href="#fn-450" id="fnref-450">450</a></sup></p>
<p>Choosing to remain in the U.S. may inevitably lead to an awful choice: capitulate to political pressure for influence over the encyclopedia, or be subject to significant penalties for non-compliance, including the loss of 501(c)(3) status.</p>
<p>As a result, we believe the Wikimedia foundation has no choice but to leave the U.S., and to choose a jurisdiction that will uphold its impartiality and values.</p>
<p>It is extremely common for international charities to live in relatively neutral territory, such as the Netherlands, Belgium, or Switzerland, all of which have a good reputation for government non-interference in their activities. We believe these would be good homes for such an important institution as Wikipedia.</p>
<h3 id="s7-1-4">7.1.4 Increase Transparency Related to Influence Attacks</h3>
<p>As Congress and other governmental agencies have attacked Wikipedia, we notice that Wikimedia’s responses to the government influence attacks have not been made public. Given the request by members of Congress for personally identifying information about Wikipedia editors, this lack of transparency is concerning.</p>
<p>While we understand that organizations must sometimes make difficult choices between the need for transparency, and pragmatic factors, we would hope that more transparency would alleviate the public’s fears about the U.S. government influencing Wikipedia on behalf of Israel and other aligned actors.</p>
<p>The Wikipedia community must be informed of pressure to manipulate Wikipedia, both from the outside, as well as from within Wikipedia. If changes are made in response to these attacks, the Wikipedia community must be able to see not just the external influence attacks, but also internal conversations which attempted to support that attack.</p>
<p>We note in particular the new ADL report’s claim of changes due to “enforcement work by Wikipedia’s NPOV-policy editors”. Wikimedia should clarify whether new NPOV-policy has been created in response to the ADL and Congressional attacks, especially on the topic of Palestine-Israel, and transparently share with the community how and what this means.</p>
<h3 id="s7-1-5">7.1.5 Strengthen ArbCom</h3>
<p>From our analysis in <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-3#s3-16">Section 3.16</a>, it seems that pro-Israel editors and external resources are gaming ArbCom, using considerable resources and research to launch the attack.</p>
<p>It seems that ArbCom, who are volunteers, were not equipped to stand up to such a dedicated one-sided assault on the arbitration process, leading to a rushed judgement and uneven application of the rules.</p>
<p>The outcome of PIA5 will likely upset the delicate balance of the PIA editors, and could lead to a significant downgrade in the quality of information shared about Palestine and Israel—indeed it seems from the ADL’s claims, this is already happening.</p>
<p>We believe that in response to this, and to prepare for potential future state-sponsored attacks on ArbCom, that ArbCom must be strengthened. However, we also believe that doing so risks introducing space for further governmental and pro-Israel influence to insert itself into Wikipedia.</p>
<p>Thus, our recommendation here is to ArbCom specifically, and involves the creation of a process that could withstand such an attack in the future. We believe that ArbCom must be funded, and in a way in which Wikimedia cannot exert influence. To do so, we wonder if Wikimedia might endow a separate trust to fund ArbCom.</p>
<h3 id="s7-1-6">7.1.6 Jimmy Wales should step down from the Board of Trustees</h3>
<p>In <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-3#s3-17">Section 3.17</a>, we discuss how Jimmy Wales abused his power to advocate to editorial change based on his strong pro-Israel biases, attempting to use his influence as founder and Board member to change a scholarly consensus. His aggressive tone and repeated citations of his power are not diminished by his later saying that he is writing on a personal basis.</p>
<p>His abuse of power violated both the Universal Wikipedia Code of Conduct and the Wikimedia Board of Trustees Code of Conduct. It also showed incredibly poor judgement.</p>
<p>Further, it violated the policy that the Wikimedia Foundation cited in response to their October 2025 letter to Senator Ted Cruz, sent just a month before Wales’ actions, in which they stated that the Wikimedia Foundation has no editorial control over Wikipedia.</p>
<p>Worse, Wales did not back down, apologize, or give any indication that he had made a mistake. If anything, his subsequent interviews indicated his belief that he approached it correctly and would do it again (see discussion in <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-3#s3-17">Section 3.17</a>).</p>
<p>We are in a world where significant influence attacks are being waged on Wikipedia, in which Wales’ actions are a liability. It is also possible that Wales is participating in an “inside-out” attack, as we discussed in <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-7#s7-1-2">7.1.2</a>. Either way, Wikimedia Foundation’s strong policy requiring a hands-off approach to editorial control by the Foundation is undermined by Wales’ actions.</p>
<p>We recommend that Jimmy Wales step down from the board. We also recommend that he deactivate his Wikipedia account, as its use confers significant power. If he continues to edit Wikipedia, he should do it from an anonymous account.</p>
<h3 id="s7-1-7">7.1.7 Clarify Israeli-influence on the Foundation</h3>
<p>In Section <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-4#s4-6">4.6</a>, we reviewed the appointment of Wikimedia Foundation’s new CEO, Bernadette Meehan, and outlined how her long career as a U.S. diplomat indicates that she may be a biased actor. In particular, as U.S. Ambassador to Chile she took part in many pro-Israel events, including hosting the ADL at her home, and receiving an award from an explicitly zionist organization.</p>
<p>We question whether Meehan was suggested as a CEO candidate by pro-Israel influence, or whether the Wikimedia Foundation’s Board of Trustees may have chosen someone who might be seen to appease the Israel Lobby or their allies in Congress. We also question whether the pro-Israel Jimmy Wales, a member of the Board of Trustees who chose Meehan, may have had a hand in her selection.</p>
<p>This potential for influence is extremely troubling for the neutrality of the organization, and potentially ties the supposedly neutral Wikipedia closer to U.S government interests.</p>
<p>We recommend that Wikimedia demonstrate otherwise, in particular through these, and potentially other, actions demonstrating its neutrality and non-alignment to its community:</p>
<ul>
<li><p>The Foundation should hire an independent auditor to assess how the CEO was chosen by the Board, and whether they were influenced by the ADL and Congressional attacks. This report should be reviewed by independent media, and published for the community.</p></li>
<li><p>Meehan should issue a statement distancing herself from her actions as U.S. Ambassador, and clarifying that she holds no personal pro-Israel biases. This should include whether she kept the Macabbe Spirit award given to her by CJCh, and if not, when she disposed of it.</p></li>
<li><p>The Foundation should make a commitment not to meet with the ADL, the Heritage Foundation, or other actors outlined in this report.</p></li>
<li><p>The Foundation should declare publicly that they will resist the influence attacks from Israel and their U.S. allies.</p></li>
</ul>
<h2 id="s7-2">7.2 Recommendations for the U.S. Government</h2>
<p>As we noted previously, members of Congress have taken extraordinary steps in effort to exert Israeli influence over Wikipedia. We believe that in some cases, these actions represent deliberate collusion to further the goals of Israeli propaganda, and, at times, due to naive politicians and staffers who remained uneducated on the issue.</p>
<p>The ADL’s report functions as propaganda, and in many cases its claims collapse under basic scrutiny. In addition, the ADL’s advocacy for Israel should be well-known to politicians and their staff, so their willingness to work with the ADL in this capacity becomes even more blatant. Participating in an orchestrated state-sponsored attack against an institution such as Wikipedia should be a disqualifying event.</p>
<p>Members of Congress who are quoted in the report, and who worked with the ADL on these investigations, show themselves as biased in coordinating to align with a foreign government, and at the expense of Americans’ interests.</p>
<p><strong>Recommendation:</strong> We recommend that Congress investigate representatives Wasserman Schultz, Comer, and Mace, to learn how they came to begin these investigations, and to better understand why they are attempting to influence Wikipedia to support such pro-Israel narratives, which themselves run counter to the ideals of the United States.</p>
<p><strong>Recommendation:</strong> We also recommend that Congress launch an investigation into the ADL as an organization, as well as its lobbying, in order to learn more about their coordination with Israel and Israeli officials to promote propaganda in the United States. We believe such actions would be disallowed under 501(c)(3) exemptions.</p>
<p><strong>Recommendation:</strong> We recognize that the members of Congress, Comer, Mace, Wasserman Schultz, and the 22 other signatories of her letter, have taken ADL propaganda at face value. We recommend that other members of Congress learn from this error, and sever ties with the ADL, as many civil rights organizations have already.<sup class="fn-ref"><a href="#fn-451" id="fnref-451">451</a></sup></p>
<p><strong>Recommendation:</strong> We recommend that the U.S. government, as well as state and international governments with strong privacy laws, investigate the ADL’s mass-surveillance to determine whether it has broken any of their laws.</p>
<h2 id="s7-3">7.3 Recommendations to LLM providers</h2>
<p>LLMs already display significant bias against Palestinians and against Palestinian human rights. A team at Google Deepmind developed a technique that analyzed when LLMs “hedged” or provided “non-affirmative” responses to prompts asking about the human rights of various racial and ethnic groups. In their 2025 pre-print, “Do LLMs exhibit demographic parity in responses to queries about Human Rights”, Palestinians experienced the worst “hedging” behaviour across all models.<sup class="fn-ref"><a href="#fn-452" id="fnref-452">452</a></sup> The 2026 follow-up paper, published in a prestigious peer-reviewed ACM conference, determined that Palestine was the most frequently discriminated against country/ethnicity.<sup class="fn-ref"><a href="#fn-453" id="fnref-453">453</a></sup></p>
<p>The ADL is not quiet about their goals. The ADL seeks to hide the reality of Israel’s actions, wherein they are responsible for the colonization of Palestine, and the ongoing forced displacement, apartheid, and violence, including genocide, against Palestinians. To hide such realities, the ADL has attempted to establish that an entire section of Wikipedia is biased, in order for it to be removed from LLMs. And instead of reliance on Wikipedia, the ADL recommends that publications with much lower standards than Wikipedia, such as Reuters, be used.</p>
<p>However, the fact that experts in antisemitism and Wikipedia, such as Dr. Shira Klein, believes that Wikipedia is actually quite reliable, stating, “[Wikipedia] is actually a pretty trustworthy place to get information on Israel-Palestine”, reiterates the reliability of Wikipedia as a source of information. As Dr. Klein says, the attacks by the ADL and Congress “purport to protect Jews, but in fact have nothing to do with antisemitism, and everything to do with controlling the narrative about Israel”.<sup class="fn-ref"><a href="#fn-454" id="fnref-454">454</a></sup></p>
<p><strong>Recommendation</strong>: Be aware that LLMs are a major target for state-sponsored influence attacks, and to develop strategies to counter them. In particular, disregard any suggestions from known members of “The Israel Lobby”, or, similarly, other lobbies, or those seeking to promote propaganda, and end all contracts and shared work with the ADL.</p>
<p><strong>Recommendation:</strong> To counter Israel’s attempts to inject influence into sources used by LLMs, it is critical to heavily cite reputable and reliable academic reports, books on Palestine and Israel, as well as reports from organizations such as the United Nations, and other reputable civil society organizations such as non-governmental organizations, think tanks, and non-profit organizations focused on human rights reports, to directly counter attacks.</p>
<p><strong>Recommendation:</strong> Beware of “inside-outside” attacks, as discussed previously in <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-7#s7-1">Section 7.1</a>.</p>
<h2 id="s7-4">7.4 Recommendations for Media</h2>
<p>A shameful number of news outlets reported on the attacks on Wikipedia in a way that omitted key context for their audiences to understand the wider situation. Only one media organization delved into the ADL report in any way, and none made a systematic attempt to verify its claims. Few media organizations even seemed to consider the reliability and verity of the unsubstantiated evidence the ADL provided.</p>
<p>Similarly, we did not find any mainstream organization who qualified their discussion of the report with the important context that the ADL is a self-described “Zionist” organization, one that it is a known part of the Israel Lobby, and that a significant amount of the civil rights organizations in the U.S. have described it as untrustworthy.</p>
<p>Rather, the report was presented at face-value, as a genuine attempt at uncovering bias, rather than as a shameful piece of propaganda attempting to influence Wikipedia and AI. This is shocking in light of the ADL documenting their goals extensively in the introduction to their report.</p>
<p>And further, no mainstream organization noted that the Congressional influence attacks on Wikipedia were launched by recipients of significant AIPAC donations. This was a significant omission as it reveals key motivation for their actions.</p>
<p>By platforming the attack without context, these media outlets become a part of the problem. Whether through lax editorial standards, deliberate participation in the bias, or sheer laziness, the public should be able to expect better from the media. But, sadly, and as discussed previously, when it comes to the topic of Palestine, the media has largely exhibited bias against Palestinians.<sup class="fn-ref"><a href="#fn-455" id="fnref-455">455</a></sup></p>
<p><strong>Recommendation:</strong> Media organizations should, as Wikipedia does, treat the ADL as unreliable on issues related to antisemitism, and specifically on Israel and Palestine.</p>
<p>In addition, all reports on the ADL should clarify that the ADL is a member of the Israel Lobby, that it considers itself to be a “Zionist” organization, and should operate under the context that the ADL’s goals are predominantly to shield Israel from criticism.</p>
<p>Reporting on topics related to “antisemitism” should provide the context that the ADL’s definition of antisemitism includes legitimate criticism of Israel, including critiquing Israel for its ongoing crimes against humanity in its violent occupation of Palestine. Further, it should be recognized that this definition of antisemitism they rely upon is not recognized by the majority of civil rights organizations.</p>
<p>Reports pertaining to civil rights should offer the context that other civil society organizations do not consider the ADL to be a serious proponent of civil rights, and that the ADL has frequently taken anti-humanitarian stances, including antisemitic stances, where it suited their purpose.</p>
<p>And finally, we call upon media organizations to recognize that the ADL is a propaganda organization, and to stop participating in ADL briefings, and spreading ADL “reports”, talking points, or other propaganda.</p>
<p><strong>Recommendation:</strong> Media organizations should note the AIPAC donations received by members of Congress, and especially when the discussion pertains to Israel, Palestine, or any related discussions (such as claimed antisemitism on college campuses or at protests).</p>
<h2 id="s7-5">7.5 Recommendations for Civil Rights Organizations</h2>
<p>The attack by the Israel Lobby against Wikipedia will require civil rights organizations to combat misinformation and propaganda on multiple fronts, especially as LLMs and media platforms rely on Wikipedia as a source of information.</p>
<p><strong>Recommendation:</strong> We call on all civil rights organizations in the U.S. to stand up for Wikipedia. If you allow Wikipedia to be weaponized against Palestine, you will soon find that it is weaponized against your cause as well.</p>
<h2 id="s7-6">7.6 Recommendations to the ADL</h2>
<p>In an age of genuine far-right antisemitism, the ADL has focused its efforts not on fighting antisemitism, but on undermining and attempting to weaken the Palestinian liberation movement. In doing so, it has violated its own anti-defamation mandate, used antisemitic slurs against anti-Zionist Jews, lost the allyship of a substantial number of civil rights organizations, and harnessed government resources in an attempt to influence Wikipedia.</p>
<p>And beyond this, ADL CEO Greenblatt has become a right-wing propagandist himself, for example, evident through moves to align himself with, and curry favor with Elon Musk, and creating genuine violence amongst student protesters at UCLA.</p>
<p>In <a href="/wikipedia-report-2026/section-5">Section 5</a>, we outlined numerous problematic actions of the ADL that occurred under Greenblatt’s leadership, including:</p>
<ul>
<li><p>The #DropTheADL campaign, where 287 civil society organizations accused the ADL of abandoning its anti-hate work</p></li>
<li><p>Undermining the entire concept of antisemitism in its annual “Antisemitism Audit”, by redefining it to include criticism of Israel</p></li>
<li><p>The ADL’s ban from Wikipedia, which relied largely on statements from Greenblatt</p></li>
<li><p>The ADL’s removal of its own “Glossary of Hate” at the request of far-right extremists</p></li>
<li><p>The ADL dropping its anti-bias education in order to focus on “antisemitism”</p></li>
<li><p>A range of public resignations from across its organization, including its high-profile head of the Center for Technology &amp; Society, Yaël Eisenstat</p></li>
<li><p>Its demands that AirBNB reinstate apartheid apartment rentals in the illegally occupied West Bank</p></li>
<li><p>Attempts to curry favor and further align with Elon Musk after his Nazi salutes</p></li>
<li><p>Praising ICE deportations of pro-Palestine students</p></li>
<li><p>Calling on NYPD to attack students</p></li>
<li><p>Referring to the Jewish civil rights group Jewish Voice for Peace as a “hate group”</p></li>
<li><p>Lying about NYC mayor-elect Mamdani, and setting up a “Mamdani monitor”</p></li>
<li><p>Launching a series of unhinged claims, including that students were Iranian proxies, comparing anti-Zionism to white supremacy, comparing keffiyehs to the swastika, claiming that the phrase “Free Palestine” is an actual threat of violence to Jewish students</p></li>
<li><p>Calling human rights impact assessments “antisemitic”, and referring to groups that promoted these assessments, such as the Franciscan Sisters of Allegany NY, and the Sisters of St. Francis of Philadelphia, as antisemitic.</p></li>
</ul>
<p>Through our findings, we recognize the ADL reports as shameful pieces of propaganda. The ADL has long been recognized as lobbyists, and have a long history of racist behaviour, and it is clear that the ADL has betrayed its original stated purpose, if even it ever took it seriously. Our belief is that the ADL, including Greenblatt, have become what they have claimed to oppose: hate-mongers and proponents of ethnic violence.</p>
<p><strong>Recommendation:</strong> We do not believe the ADL can seriously reform itself. As such, ADL partners should distance themselves from the organization, lest they help the ADL spread hate. Donors should stop donating, ADL employees should find new jobs, and board members should resign. ADL chapters should be shut down or be broken off.</p>
<p><strong>Recommendation:</strong> We recommend in particular for the anti-Zionist Jewish community to push back on the ADL as it tries to destroy Wikipedia’s neutrality for its own pro-Israel gains. In particular, anti-Zionists could work to reduce any funding which goes to the ADL from their families, friends, institutions, or community.</p>
