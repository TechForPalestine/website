<p>This document uses the following policy for responsible links and citations. Where possible:</p>
<ul>
<li><p>Links to content controlled by Zionist or pro-Israel organizations use archive.org links instead of direct links where possible, so that the content cannot be removed or altered</p></li>
<li><p>Links to paywalled content use archived links, to provide access for researchers and readers</p></li>
<li><p>Links to Wikipedia use Wikipedia’s “permalink” feature, to provide access to the actual content referred to in the report</p></li>
</ul>
