T4P can support your initiative in donations in a number of ways. To be eligible, you should be in Good Standing, and the grant or donation must be for non-profit purposes.

## Good standing

Projects must be in “Good standing”. This largely means that you’ve taken part in the incubator (attended Strategy Calls, Onboarding, and Office Hours). We also expect you to have kept us up-to-date on their progress, and attend office hours at least once a month.

## Non-profit

T4P can only provide grants for non-profit purposes, and the grants cannot be used for for-profit activities. This typically means you need to be a non-profit organization, or unincorporated. For profit companies can still get grants under some circumstances, but only if the grant’s intention is a covered non-profit purpose and does not provide a “private benefit”. Contact us with your use case for advice.

## Microgrants

Tech for Palestine has started providing modest funding - a credit card to pay for expenses, starting at $300/month. Reach out to @\_grants in your private Discord channel to apply. We have a simple form to fill out and a legal agreement to sign.

## Fiscal Sponsorship

If you have external donors, they may seek to donate via a “Fiscal sponsor” - a 501c3 org through which they can receive tax deductions for their donation. Tech for Palestine can fulfil this role for you.

If your project is not “in good standing”, then we might choose not to be your Fiscal Sponsor. Please check before providing this information to your donor.

The T4P Legal Intake Team is made up of Palestinian and pro-Palestinian lawyers who have volunteered their time _pro bono_ (for free) to support the Tech for Palestine community and T4P projects! You can email them at: legal-intake@techforpalestine.org

The Legal Intake team includes lawyers (or are connected to lawyers) across a wide range of practice areas relevant to tech, including tax, employment, company formation and structuring, intellectual property, sanctions, cybersecurity, privacy, data protection, international law, technology transactions, finance, contract, product counseling, policy review, investment, fundraising matters, and other legal topics that commonly arise as organizations and products grow.

Specifically, this may include things like drafting privacy policies, reviewing terms of service and platform policies, reviewing term sheets, advising on corporate structures, editing partnership and shareholder agreements, counseling on legal and regulatory risks, preparing for product launches, reviewing vendor or customer contracts, advising on regulatory considerations, helping structure fundraising rounds, assessing compliance obligations, supporting negotiations with partners or investors, reviewing licensing and distribution arrangements, advising on employment and contractor relationships, developing internal governance policies, preparing for diligence processes, assisting with cross-border expansion issues, and helping leaders think through legal strategy as their organizations scale.

**Importantly, members of the Legal Intake Committee are not acting as lawyers for Tech for Palestine, incubator participants, partners, or for anyone else who uses this service, and their participation does not create an attorney-client relationship. They are volunteers who are willing, when appropriate, to share knowledge or help point participants in the right direction. Any formal legal representation or engagement would need to be arranged independently.**
