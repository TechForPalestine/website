At Office Hours, you'll be asked to give an update on what's going on. Sometimes you may send the update by email, Discord, or ProjectHub. Here are some tips on those updates.

The main content of the update should be about your progress towards your goals. Specific actions, features, team updates, etc, are not super helpful unless related to the goal, or related to a question you have

Here's an example of a bad update:

- We added a new feature to frogosticate the battalions. Our UI needed a bit of a polish and we're working on that. Also added a new team member on community. Yeah, so that's what we've been up to. Oh, can you introduce us to some journalists?

Here's a good version of this:

- As we mentioned before, we recently launched and had really good feedback from current users, and so right now our top priority is growth off the back of that launch. We have 6000 users and our growth rate is 6% per week, we'd like to increase that to 10% this month. Our main issue is that new user conversion is around 3%, and we think we can increase that a lot with some new stuff.
- User feedback from churned new users has informed us that they are confused by the UI, and that they expected to be able to frognosticate their battalions, which was on our homepage and our roadmap but hasn't been released yet. We're about to release a fix to the UI based on a user study we just finished, and the frognosticate feature is being tested with some users now. We expect they might need a few tweaks but we think this will double our conversation rate, and we should know within 2 weeks.
- Also, we identified that users don't understand that there's a community forum that they can be part of that can really help with their work - that makes sense as we've been neglecting it due to capacity, so we just brought on a new volunteer to manage it.
- Question: if the frognosticate feature works, and our conversion rate does go above 5%, we believe we'll be in a great position for media attention. Are you connected to any journalists in our space?

Why is this better? It starts with explaining your key goals at the moment, and tying them to your overall goals. Then it ties each update to the goals that you've just explained. This is also a helpful format to think about when working on understanding what you've achieved internally, for your own notes or for communicating with your team.
