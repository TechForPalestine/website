If our incubator's projects are growing, it means that T4P is growing as well. Our Marketing team is therefore always ready to assist.

Reach out to marketing@techforpalestine.org in these situations

- You need advice/feedback on your growth strategy
- You simply need us to reshare your social media posts
- You have news that you need us to amplify
- You need our help with graphic design or copy writing
- Feedback on/help with website copy
- Anything else that feels like a marketing thing

In case you want to do a campaign regarding a (soft) project launch, make sure you have a 'go' from our incubator team first.
