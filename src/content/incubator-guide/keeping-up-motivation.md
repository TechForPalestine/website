Please remember that the fate of Palestine does not rest on your shoulders. You are a cog in a big machine, and that machine is itself a cog in a much bigger machine. You cannot accept responsibility for every terrible thing that happens. Your place in the movement is getting your project to succeed.

If you see an atrocity, your best way to deal with that is to get back to work. You cannot solve it directly, you can only work on the work you do. If you had an army, or a billion dollars, or ran Meta, or were a president, you might address it differently. But you don’t, you’re addressing it with your project.

So when you see something which needs to be called out, for sure, repost something. Support allies and colleagues. But do not take on the responsibility for not having solved a problem that started before you were born.

It is helpful to spend less time looking at the genocide. I know they say “don’t look away” and it feels like at least paying witness to what they’re experiencing is valuable. And it is. But you can’t help Palestine if you’re an emotional mess. You need to develop a certain detached attitude to it, as awful as that is to say. Perhaps take a break from social media. Focus on the good you can do with your project — even if it doesn’t help today, this is how we make change.

Remember that Palestine needs you to keep going for the long term.
