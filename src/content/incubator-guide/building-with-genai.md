GenAI is now at the stage where the best tools can now build significant and complex apps without the need for a dedicated engineering team. While this is still much easier for those with a technical background (so keep your eng team if you have one), it is also at the place where those without a technical background can make significant progress in app building, and would be very suitable for many of you.

The tools you want to look into are Claude Code, and repl.it. From feedback I've heard, both of these are now at the stage where the product owner can tell the AI what you want the app to do, and the GenAI will make a plan, and iteratively build features with you. For simple websites, I've also heard good things about loveable.

The takeaway here is that you should all be using these tools, and your dev teams too. They are far far too powerful to ignore, especially considering the limited time volunteers have. A feature which would take 20 hours (stretched over 2 months) can be finished in 20 minutes in many cases.

Most importantly, we have had multiple project leaders who are not engineers who have been able to build sites and apps using these tools, without an engineering team. This is actually much preferable to working with engineers, as there is not a need for nearly as much communication and there is no misunderstanding how things work, or need to wait for feedback.

When and if your project gets to the stage where it needs some specialized skills to host the app (eg devops or cloud skills) we may be able to find you a volunteer or lend a staff engineer.
